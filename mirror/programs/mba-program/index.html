Program Positioning

In the AI-powered era, anyone can become the CEO of a one-person company. AI does not only empower businesses but also fuels mass innovation and entrepreneurship. HKUST(GZ) MBA in Innovation and Entrepreneurship (MBA+) is positioned to be the Accelerator for AI Business Leaders, helping them to achieve skill, value and network acceleration.

Skill Acceleration

Harness the power of AI to internalize data-driven decision-making and automation as core competencies, shifting from traditional management to intelligent strategic oversight.

Value Acceleration

Leverage the wisdom of scientists to translate frontier technologies from world-class labs to commercial impact, shortening the distance between the experiment and the market.

Network Acceleration

Build on the prestige of HKUST to efficiently earn a degree from a top-tier QS ranking university, deeply connect with a global alumni network, and establish a strategic hub for personal development and elite social mobility.
